# Carnival & Haldi artwork

Final project asset: `assets/carnival-haldi.webp`.

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. The existing `assets/wedding-mandap.png` was supplied as a style reference, not an edit target. The resulting original portrait illustration was converted to WebP for the invitation. It depicts a decorative illustrated couple rather than verified likenesses of Jenny and Priyansh. Animation is implemented in HTML with a gentle camera move and drifting marigold petals.

## Final generation prompt

Create a refined Indian wedding invitation illustration, tall portrait 9:16 layout. Art direction: delicate hand-painted watercolor on ivory cotton paper, softly washed edges, detailed but light and airy luxury Indian wedding stationery, pale butter yellow, warm marigold, muted sage green, blush accents, gold architectural detail. It must visually belong beside the supplied watercolor wedding mandap illustration, not be a poster or photograph. This is a completely new CARNIVAL AND HALDI scene. Entire upper 38% is clean warm ivory lightly textured empty space reserved for editable HTML text, absolutely no lettering or logos anywhere. Only a few delicate long yellow marigold strings at the extreme upper left and right edges. Lower 62% depicts an elegant open Rajasthani palace garden courtyard in daytime, an Indian bride in butter-yellow lehenga with floral jewellery and groom in ivory kurta with yellow shawl seated together on two low carved stools, joyfully applying a small touch of haldi to each other's cheek with believable graceful hands. Decorative stylized illustrated faces and flat flowing fabric, no photorealism, matching traditional invitation illustrations. Symmetrical marigold-flower arch and scalloped ivory palace niches behind them, charming pastel carnival umbrellas and a small subtle vintage Ferris wheel in the far background at the right, tasteful fabric bunting, brass haldi bowls and flower-filled urlis in the foreground, potted marigold and sage foliage, a few watercolor petals. Festive, intimate, sophisticated and richly layered. Couple is the focal point within the lower half, whole seated figures visible, no crop on their heads or hands. Background and architectural elements blend with the ivory paper with no hard rectangle boundaries. No text, no typography, no watermark, no borders. Produce a finished high quality watercolor asset suitable for full-screen mobile invitation.
